# Changelog

## [2.0.0] - 2026-05-29

### Added
- Four-tab layout: Overview, Regions, Industry, Trends
- Regional unemployment heatmap across 8 Finnish regions 2015-2025
- Per-region sparklines with trajectory and change from baseline
- Region ranking bar chart for 2025
- Stacked employment by industry 2015-2023
- Industry-level unemployment rates for 2025 with red/amber/green severity coding
- Shortage occupation panel with severity coding
- Finland vs EU27 comparison line chart
- Percentage-point change chart per region 2019 to 2025
- Structural context cards with April 2026 monthly reading and EC forecast
- Live data badge showing data currency
- Year-from filter and metric toggle
- Full green colour palette

### Data updated
- 2025 national rate: 9.7% (Statistics Finland LFS Jan 2026 release)
- 2025 youth rate: 21.5% (World Bank WDI / OECD)
- 2025 adult 25+ rate: 8.1% (OECD LRHUADTTFIA156S)
- 2025 employment rate: 76.0% age 20-64 (Statistics Finland)
- April 2026 monthly: 11.6% (Statistics Finland)
- EC Spring 2026 forecast: 10.1% for 2026, 9.8% for 2027
